## English

Name: Nest Player

Subtitle: Your media. Every room.

Description:

Play your own music, podcasts, and video soundtracks on a Google Cast speaker
from a native Mac player. Build a playlist from local files or a network folder,
watch supported video on your Mac, and keep playback controls on your iPhone.

- Add files and folders to a persistent, reorderable playlist.
- Use a Finder-mounted SMB share as part of your media library.
- Send audio to a compatible Cast speaker on the same local network.
- Keep Mac audio muted or enable sound from both outputs.
- Scan a QR code to open the paired browser remote on your phone.

A compatible Cast device and a reachable local network are required. Picture
playback depends on supported macOS codecs. Nest Player is an independent
project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Keywords: cast,audio,player,speaker,playlist,local,video,network,nas,remote

## 繁體中文（台灣）

名稱：Nest Player

副標題：你的媒體，每個房間都聽得見。

說明：

用 Mac 原生播放器，將自己的音樂、Podcast 與影片聲音播放到相容的 Google Cast
喇叭。從本機檔案或網路資料夾建立播放清單，在 Mac 看畫面，再用 iPhone 控制。

- 加入檔案與資料夾，保存並重新排序播放清單。
- 從 Finder 掛載的 SMB 共享資料夾讀取媒體。
- 將音訊送到同一區網的相容 Cast 喇叭。
- Mac 音訊可保持靜音，或開啟雙輸出。
- 掃描 QR Code，在手機瀏覽器開啟已配對的遙控器。

需要相容的 Cast 裝置與可互相連線的區域網路。影片畫面支援取決於 macOS 的解碼
能力。Nest Player 為獨立專案，與 Google 無隸屬或背書關係。

關鍵字：音樂,播放器,喇叭,播放清單,區域網路,影片,網路硬碟,遙控,音訊
